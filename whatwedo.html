What we do.

Golden Agro Foodz is a farm-to-kitchen food brand from Baba Bakala Sahib, near Amritsar in Punjab. We make raw honey in five varieties, cold-pressed mustard oil, farm-grown turmeric powder and honey rose gulkand, and every batch carries a batch number you can quote when you order.

Raw honey

Five varieties. Swipe the photo to see each one.

Current batch: 05

We gather our honey from different regions, so each variety tastes of the place it comes from. Multiflora honey comes from Punjab and Himachal. Jamun and Ajwain honey come from parts of Punjab. Kashmiri Acacia honey comes from Kashmir, and Black Forest honey comes from the deep forests of Himachal. All five are raw honey and are packed in clean sealed jars.

Our farms are registered with the National Bee Board. Multiflora honey is graded A+ under Agmark and our other honeys are graded A. Honey is a natural sweetener and many families use it in tea, with warm water and lemon, on parathas and toast, or in sweets in place of sugar. Pick the variety you like by swiping the photo above.

Mustard oil

Golden mustard oil for everyday cooking.

Current batch: 01

Our mustard oil is made from mustard seed grown in the fields of Hanumangarh. We press it cold on our own farm, so the oil keeps the strong, true aroma that Punjabi kitchens expect, and it is filled into clean sealed bottles.

Cold-pressed mustard oil is the traditional oil for tadka, sabzi, parathas and homemade pickles. Order a 1 litre bottle for the kitchen, or a 2 or 5 litre pack for a larger family or a small eatery.

Turmeric powder

Deep golden haldi, ground fine.

Current batch: H03

Our turmeric is grown on our own farms and grown organically. After harvest we dry the turmeric and prepare it into powder by the traditional method, then pack it fine and fresh. It has the deep golden colour and warm aroma of real haldi.

Turmeric is used every day in Indian cooking: dal, sabzi, rice and the golden milk many people drink at night. We pack it in 500g and 1kg sizes for homes, shops and small kitchens.

Honey Rose Gulkand

Rose petals and our multiflora honey in a 400g jar.

Current batch: 02

Our Honey Rose Gulkand is made from our multiflora honey and flowers from the same farm where we collect the honey. The flower petals are slowly mixed with honey into a sweet, fragrant preserve.

Gulkand is a traditional Indian favourite that people enjoy as a cooling treat in summer. Have a spoonful on its own, spread it on toast or bread, or stir it into cold milk. It comes in a 400g jar.

Questions we get asked

Where does your honey come from?

Multiflora honey comes from Punjab and Himachal. Jamun and Ajwain honey come from parts of Punjab, Kashmiri Acacia honey comes from Kashmir, and Black Forest honey comes from the deep forests of Himachal.

Is your honey tested?

Yes. Our honey is tested at a NABL-accredited laboratory and has passed all the tests, including NMR. Our farms are registered with the National Bee Board, Multiflora honey is Agmark grade A+ and our other honeys are grade A.

How is your mustard oil made?

It is made from mustard grown in the fields of Hanumangarh and cold pressed on our own farm.

How is your turmeric powder made?

The turmeric is grown organically on our own farms. We dry it and prepare it into powder by the traditional method.

What is Honey Rose Gulkand made from?

It is made from our multiflora honey and flowers from the farm where we collect the honey.
